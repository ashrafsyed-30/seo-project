Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Introduction to SEO

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the process of improving a website so that it can appear higher in search engine results. The main goal of SEO is to help more people find a website through unpaid or organic search results. SEO has been around since the 1990s, when search engines first started becoming popular. Today, SEO is an important part of digital marketing because it can help websites get more visitors without paying for every click. SEO focuses on organic search results, while PPC (Pay-Per-Click) is used for paid advertisements. Both SEO and PPC are important parts of Search Engine Marketing (SEM).

Key Terms of SEO

  • SEO - Search Engine Optimization
  • Organic Traffic - Visitors who came from unpaid search results
  • Keyword - A word or phrase people type into a search engine
  • PPC - Paid advertising on search engines

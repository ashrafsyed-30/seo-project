Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Introduction to SEO

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the process of improving a website so people can find it more easily through search engines. It focuses on unpaid search results, which are also called organic search results.

SEO is useful for businesses, organizations, and people who want their websites to reach the right audience. A website with clear information and useful content can give visitors a better experience.

Key Terms of SEO

  • SEO: Improving a website for search engines.
  • Organic Traffic: Visitors who come from unpaid search results.
  • Keyword: A word or phrase people search for online.
  • PPC: Pay-Per-Click advertising, where advertisers pay for clicks.

SEO in Real Life

Imagine a student searching for "Indian restaurants near Kennesaw State University." The student wants to find a nearby restaurant, check the menu, and see its opening hours.

A restaurant website can help by providing clear information about its food, location, hours, and contact details. SEO helps organize this information so visitors and search engines can understand the page.

Types of SEO

SEO Type Main Purpose Example
On-Page SEO Improve webpage content. Useful headings and page titles.
Technical SEO Help search engines access and understand a website. Mobile-friendly pages and XML sitemaps.
Local SEO Help businesses reach people in a specific area. Restaurant location and business hours.

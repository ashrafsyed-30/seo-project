On-Page SEO

What is On-Page SEO?

If someone searches “Best Indian Restaurants near Kennesaw State University,” an Indian restaurant can improve its visibility by using a clear page title, useful headings, information about its menu and location, descriptive images, and relevant keywords in the content.

Real-Life Example of On-Page SEO

On-page SEO can be seen on many restaurant websites. For example, an Indian restaurant near Kennesaw State University can use specific keywords and useful information to help both customers and search engines understand the page.

Using Relevant Keywords

A restaurant could naturally use phrases such as “Indian restaurant near Kennesaw State University” and “Indian food in Kennesaw” in its content. The keywords should fit naturally instead of being repeated too many times.

Clear Page Titles

Instead of using a general title such as “Home,” a restaurant could use a more descriptive title such as “Indian Restaurant Near Kennesaw State University.”

Useful Headings

Headings such as “Our Menu,” “Popular Indian Dishes,” “Location,” and “Restaurant Hours” make the page easier for visitors to understand and organize the information clearly.

Optimized Images

A restaurant can use descriptive image names and alt text. For example, an image of a biryani dish could have alt text such as “Chicken biryani at an Indian restaurant in Kennesaw.”

Helpful Content

The website can provide useful information such as the menu, location, business hours, popular dishes, and directions. This gives visitors the information they are actually looking for.

Why On-Page SEO Matters

On-page SEO helps a website communicate clearly with both visitors and search engines. When a page has useful content, clear headings, relevant keywords, descriptive images, and a good title, users can find the information they need more easily. For a local restaurant, this can help the website become more relevant to people searching for restaurants in the area.

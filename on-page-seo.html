On-Page SEO

What Is On-Page SEO?

On-page SEO means improving the content and HTML elements on a webpage. It helps visitors understand the page and helps search engines understand what the page is about.

Some common parts of on-page SEO include page titles, headings, keywords, images, and useful content.

Real-Life Example of On-Page SEO

Imagine someone searching for "Indian restaurants near Kennesaw State University." A restaurant can make its website more useful by clearly showing its menu, location, opening hours, and popular dishes.

Using Relevant Keywords

Keywords are words and phrases that people type into search engines. A restaurant might naturally use phrases such as "Indian food in Kennesaw" in its webpage content. Keywords should fit the information instead of being repeated too often.

Clear Page Titles

A page title tells people and search engines what a webpage covers. Instead of using a general title like "Home," a restaurant could use "Indian Restaurant in Kennesaw."

Useful Headings

Headings organize information into sections. Headings such as "Our Menu," "Popular Dishes," "Location," and "Opening Hours" help visitors find the information they need.

Optimized Images

Images can make a webpage more helpful and interesting. Descriptive file names and alternative text, also called alt text, help explain what an image shows.

For example, an image of a dish could use alt text such as "Chicken biryani served at an Indian restaurant."

Helpful Content

A useful restaurant website can provide its menu, location, business hours, prices, and contact information. Accurate and clear information helps visitors decide whether the restaurant meets their needs.

Meta Description

A meta description gives a short summary of a webpage. Search engines may show this summary under the page title in search results. A clear description can help people understand what they may find on the page.

Why On-Page SEO Matters

On-page SEO makes website content easier to understand and use. Clear titles, useful headings, relevant keywords, descriptive images, and helpful information can improve the experience for visitors. These practices also help search engines understand the subject of a webpage.

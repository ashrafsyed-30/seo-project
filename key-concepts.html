Key SEO Concepts

1. Keywords

Keywords are words or phrases that people type into search engines. For example, someone looking for a place to eat might search for "Indian restaurants near Kennesaw." Websites can use relevant keywords to describe their content.

2. Search Intent

Search intent is the reason behind a person's search. Someone searching for "Indian restaurants near Kennesaw" probably wants to find a nearby restaurant. A helpful webpage should provide information such as the menu, location, and opening hours.

3. Backlinks

A backlink is a link from another website to your website. For example, a local community website might link to a restaurant's website. Useful backlinks can help people discover the website and may contribute to search rankings.

4. Mobile SEO

Mobile SEO focuses on making a website work well on smartphones and other mobile devices. Text should be readable, links should be easy to tap, and pages should fit different screen sizes.

5. Local SEO

Local SEO helps businesses reach people searching in a particular area. A restaurant in Kennesaw can provide accurate location details, opening hours, and business information to help local customers find it.

6. Crawling

Crawling is the process in which search engines discover webpages by following links and using other methods. Search engine crawlers visit pages to learn what content is available on a website.

7. Indexing

Indexing happens when a search engine processes and stores information about a webpage in its index. A page generally needs to be indexed to appear in regular search results, although indexing does not guarantee that it will appear.

Why These Concepts Matter

These seven concepts explain different parts of SEO. Keywords and search intent help websites match what people are looking for. Backlinks can help people discover pages, while mobile SEO and local SEO improve the experience for different audiences. Crawling and indexing help explain how search engines discover and process webpages.
